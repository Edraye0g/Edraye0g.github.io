<!-- Biography / Statement -->
<h2 class="portfolio-section-heading"><span>🔬</span> Research Statement & Biography</h2>

<p>
  I am a Biomedical Engineering undergraduate at <strong>Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET)</strong> with a focused dedication to <strong>Computational Psychiatry</strong>, <strong>Cognitive Neuroscience</strong>, and <strong>Neuroimaging</strong>.
</p>

<p>
  My research inquiry centers on formalizing the mechanisms that govern <em>how the human brain navigates uncertainty, forms beliefs, and makes decisions</em>. By synthesizing principles from <strong>Bayesian inference</strong>, <strong>functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS)</strong>, and <strong>electrophysiological signal analysis (EEG)</strong>, I investigate the physiological and computational underpinnings of mental health dysfunctions, stress states, and cognitive biases.
</p>

<p>
  Through this academic platform, I routinely publish detailed computational deconstructions of landmark peer-reviewed research, mapping theoretical neuroscience into accessible mathematical frameworks and reproducible code.
</p>
