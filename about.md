## About Me
I’m **Rayeed Al Mutakabbir**, a biomedical engineering student with a passion for **computational psychiatry** and **neuroeconomics**.  
This blog is my space to review research papers, share insights, and explore how brain science intersects with decision-making.

## Why This Blog?
- To make complex neuroscience papers more accessible.
- To connect psychiatry with economics and computational modeling.
- To document my journey as I prepare for postgraduate studies in Europe.

## Current Focus
- Mental health.
- Decision-making under uncertainty.
- Neuroimaging tools and computational models.
