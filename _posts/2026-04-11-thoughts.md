Why are you scared? Anything can go wrong will go wrong. You have put enough effort to this exam. It’s your thing. Linear Algebra, Vector, Simulation and all. Mainly visualization. But still, you should remember your power. Theres a limit. Your limit is knowledge. Your limit is staying behind dark. Your limit is not being able to recognize by others. You also do not like it at all and its fine. You tried hard. You not being able to get 4 out of 4 doesn’t define your depth of knowledge. But the question always comes at the end of the road, and that is will you be able to find success even after all this knowledge? Its getting hard and this tough situation never ends but it’s a sign that you care. You cared and you were drop dead serious but but but…

The thing is, life is all about ifs and buts. I tried to follow knowledge. I tried to dream of being able to find a path where I will feel contained. 3 semesters have passed. Term final exam of the 4th semester is ongoing. 2 exams have been finished and the most outrageous thing is I couldn’t say for once that I have done wonder in any of it. Tomorrow, in my favorite exam, I certainly can say that I won’t be able to do good in it. Because, after sitting for almost hundreds of exams, I learnt one thing and that is I am not a good student and I cannot do good ever in any of my exams and my academic career is actually a failure. I can say, that’s an acceptance. However, this type of acceptance never does good to anyone. Rather, they make people dull and the spirit to fight for life actually brushes out of mentality. And, that happened with me. I accepted my fate. 

To be fair, my problem is I rely on my intuition. I rely on my visual memory. Not like I am bad at it, but someone close to me always said that bird’s view won’t carry you much longer. I always wanted to avoid competition, because I wanted to think different. While doing so, I came to understand that I have really surpassed myself and this understanding made me a lonely person. While trying to be mature, tough and sober, I became insane and now coming at this point, looking around, I feel like I am lost on my own thoughts and judgements. 

I am lost. In my thoughts, words, knowledge, love, pain and misery also dream and goals. My goal is to be something where I can be a free thinker, not rushing to my workplace but walking to my workplace and finding satisfaction. I want to face challenge that goes against my thoughts and coping up with it. 

But here I can see one thing. My dream is to cope up challenge. Will I be able to cope up them? I can’t even change my situation here. I am in pain, trying to get a good result tomorrow with my own way but the world doesn’t work like that. The world wants something and my type is something else and this is the biggest challenge of life. And here, I am losing. 

I really like problem solving. Learning new things. I am a fast learner. I can learn things so quickly that I can be implemented into work very efficiently. Now the biggest question arises. Who am I? I asked this question to myself very often. Gemini told me that I am a made-up character and named me Silas Vane. Sometimes I am Marcus Aurelius. Sometimes I dive deep into being a machine lover. I love what I learn. But when I look at the result, its painful and I cannot put that into words. I help people do their projects. I do many things. But I am pretty sure that I am only on an act. I could find myself one or two times and feel like yes, I have achieved it but most of the time I question myself whether its possible for me to actually gain what I want. I feel like everything will go in vain and its not possible for me to get back up. I am pumped up most of the time. But when I am at my low, I lost all my thoughts and maturities. I want to cry but only one or two drops will come which will lead to new thoughts like I am on a new act and its crybaby. Then, I will mesmerize myself with newer understanding of life and that’s it, I am in the new act of life. Heartless and sustained-pain face talking less, laughing less attitude. 

I always tell people to be optimistic; that so called BE POSITIVE attitude. But I am the most pessimistic and hateful person ever. I love when people criticize me. I take criticism as complement. I don’t think I belong to this world. I am in pain. Pressure of knowledge, the hunger for more knowledge and not being able to get more knowledge and then procrastination what saddens me the most. I needed to be more productive with my time, but lacked that. 

I love to focus only on knowledge. But does that ever make such difference? How far can knowledge take me? I think somewhere where I can be contained and satisfied. Only this optimism what pushes me wake-up everyday and do what I must do to climb at the edge and that is myself.
