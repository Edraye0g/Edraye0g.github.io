A famous hypothesis proposed by Andy Clark and David Chalmers at 1998 proposed an idea based upon cognition. They extended the understanding of mind, not only by body/brain, but also took it further towards external objects. Think of it like a blind man and his cane or a deaf man with his ear-plugs. Sounds vague but they put the hypothesis in order of the study of how we should stop everything making sense by converging everything together. If things like a cane, a diary, or a laptop can make the job done every single day like legs, eyes, and hands, why can't they be the extension of our self. This is the foundational mechanism of complexity science and cognition. 

We think brain as if it is a computer. But, computer is a linearly functional, explainable. Computer is not a complex system, rather it is a complicated system. Naming brain as a computer is called *Computationalism* or *The Computational Theory of Mind*. This idea developed at mid-20th century in analytic philosophy. The theory explains that human brain thinks like bunch of number calculation, algorithm manipulation, and data structures. This is an extension of functionalism where the system is defined by what it is doing rather than its structure.

A study from *Cognitive Science- A Multidisciplinary Journal* framed human brain as a complex system and proved a point of why we should think outside of the box in terms of computation. Complexity science integrates mathematically rigorous concepts from three tied and tested disciplines. 

 **System Theory**
 A system must follow holism. The system must share input and output relationship, boundary, and subsystems. This should contain a feedback loop for adaptive approach. 

 **Nonlinear Dynamic System Theory**
 An extensive version of system theory where the behavior of the output is unpredictable. Here, a simple input can have chaotic output or no output at all. This is a method to understand catastrophe flags. 

 **Synergetics**
 Nature has its own underlying physics. The study of any spontaneous process is called sysnergetics. If we look at water, that's a wet substance formed by hydrogen and oxygen atoms. But, individually they are not wet at all. This study helps to find the core principle of a process.

The reframing of cognitive science relies on three mathematically rigorous concepts:

**Emergence:** Occurs when the "whole is more than the sum of its parts." In cognition, this refers to phenomena best understood at scales of organization that do not reduce to constituent components.
**Nonlinearity:** A state where a system's output is not proportional to its input. This is often the result of exponential and multiplicative interactions.
**Self-Organization:** Situations where order arises without internal or external instruction (e.g., a central control unit). This is often explained through **circular causality**, where the macroscale (e.g., a pumping heart) constrains and is constrained by the microscale (e.g., valves, and chambers).

This get us back to the neuronal avalanche. A certain stimuli arrived through the environment has structured but cascading nonlinear effect on the neuronal pathway. This type of structured but nonlinear system cannot withstand any mathematically rigorous computational description like any CPU where algorithms are developed by machine code and strictly follows the protocol of the system.
