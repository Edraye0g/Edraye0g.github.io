Making decisions at an organizational level gets harder with each variable of consideration. Efficiency, time, money, distribution, fairness and many other things. Considering a psychological evaluation on Humean’s utilitarianism and Rawlsian Fairness mechanism, a study from *PNAS* journal shows the how human mind think on the basis of social welfare on an individual level vs group. The study [*Tipping the balance between fairness and efficiency through temporoparietal stimulation*](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2409395121) showed how a specific region called temporoparietal junction called on fairness for worst-off individual.
