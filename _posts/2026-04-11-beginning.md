## Background
Anxiety is a state of mind. Like happiness or agony and many other. Anxiety occurs when people is deeply in worry/unease which leads to sudden malfunction of physiological system. Rapid heartbeat, heavy sweating, restlessness, shotness of breathe etc. Occasional anxiety is common and it happens to almost everyone. However, overwhelming anxiety makes more problems in day to day life. The paper "Anxiety attenuates learning advantages conferred by statistical stability and induces loss of volatility-attuning in brain activity" [Read the paper here](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/hbm.26230) talks and compares about two distinct theories. Hypersensitivity and attenuation theory. Hypersensitivity which is supported by some literature suggests that anxiety improves the neural responses to unexpected stimuli whereas attenuation says that it is anxiety that leads to imparement of neural responses and decreases functionality hence plunges learning curve.  

![Fig. Hypersensitivity vs Attenuation](/assests/Gemini_Generated_Image_a5a4h6a5a4h6a5a4.png)

Human brain always tries to predict the next move. While driving, it is necessary otherwise there is a risk of accident. While talking to somebody, we try to predict what the person will say next. But, when our prediction comes negative that is called prediction error. This comes to us as a surprise. Using this understanding, TSAVO experiment were done on 35 individuals to understand what occurs in the human brain. In anxiety period, can human brain predict when the next possible scenario will be happening? This is the main hypothesis that was tested along the study.
